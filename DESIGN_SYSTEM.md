# Mombyeol AI Design System v1.0 Master Specification (Antigravity IDE)

> **"Follow the Star. Discover Yourself."**
> *"별은 미래를 맞추는 것이 아니라, 나를 이해하는 지도입니다."*

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Purpose
Mombyeol AI is a premium emotional AI brand designed for deep self-understanding.
- **Never present fortune-telling, prediction, or cold surveys.**
- Every interaction must feel like a warm, guided, emotional journey.
- **Emotional Feeling**: Apple × Studio Ghibli × Julian Opie × Calm App.

---

## 2. Official Brand Color Palette (Strict 5 Colors Only)
No additional colors are allowed.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Deep Navy | `#455C73` | Core text, structural anchors |
| **Secondary** | Mint | `#9FE2BF` / `#3B9C87` | Journey Line, active progress, success accents |
| **Accent** | Warm Gold | `#F6C85F` | North Star, completed milestones, certificate seals |
| **Emotion** | Coral Pink | `#FF9AA2` | Primary CTA buttons, star lantern heartbeat glow |
| **Background** | Light Cream | `#FFF9F2` / `#FFFFFF` | Card backgrounds, generous white space |

---

## 3. Brand Assets Scale & Priority (120~140% Scale Lock)
Brand assets must always be recognized visually before reading any text.

- **Logo**: 130–160% enlarge anchor (`height: 56px`)
- **North Star**: 200% enlarge anchor (`font-size: 8.5rem`, soft gold pulse glow)
- **Lantern**: Enlarge anchor with Coral Pink (`#FF9AA2`) heartbeat bling animation
- **Mint Journey Line**: Unbroken connecting line from Hero to all subsequent pages
- **AI Claire**: Upper-body portrait ONLY (`210px × 210px`, 150% enlarged circular profile)
- **Popo**: 30–40% enlarged companion carrying the official lantern
- **Fili (Pily)**: Record Keeper with notebook and pencil
- **Rookie**: AI Assistant helper
- **Pomi**: Healing companion

---

## 4. Emotional Information Fields (Mombyeol Journey Language)

### 4.1 Field Name Replacements
- `성함 / Name` ➔ **`별빛 이름`** (or `여행자 이름`)
- `연령대 / Age` ➔ **`인생의 계절`**
- `관심 분야 / Interest` ➔ **`오늘 가장 마음이 머무는 곳`**
- `고민 / Problem` ➔ **`오늘 가장 비추고 싶은 마음`**
- `시작 / Start` ➔ **`별빛 여정 시작하기 (나의 북극성 찾기)`**
- `다음 / Next` ➔ **`다음 별빛으로 →`**
- `이전 / Prev` ➔ **`← 이전 별빛`**
- `완료 / Finish` ➔ **`여정을 마치고 별빛 받기 →`**

### 4.2 Age Options (인생의 계절)
- `10대 (꿈을 찾는 계절)`
- `20대 (도전을 시작하는 계절)`
- `30대 (성장의 계절)`
- `40대 (균형의 계절)`
- `50대 (제2의 인생 준비)`
- `60대 (지혜의 계절)`
- `70+ (별빛을 나누는 계절)`

### 4.3 Interest Options (오늘 가장 마음이 머무는 곳)
- `마음 회복` / `관계` / `가족` / `진로` / `일` / `꿈` / `건강` / `새로운 시작`

## 5. Journey Progress Tracker System
- **Steps**: `🌱 First Step` ➔ `❓ Path of Questions` ➔ `🗺️ Star Map` ➔ `⭐ North Star`
- **Current Step**: Mint (`#3B9C87`)
- **Completed Step**: Warm Gold (`#F6C85F`)
- **Connecting Visual**: Glowing Mint Line extending from the North Star through the entire page flow.
